The project at a glance

A security challenge shaped by distance, darkness, and infrastructure.

Storage facilities can have wide perimeters, multiple gates, and long stretches of time without staff on site. In this published APS case study, the facilities also varied in power, connectivity, lighting, and surrounding conditions.

The challenge

Recurring break-ins, vandalism, and attempted theft created risk after hours. Operators needed visibility at entry gates, loading zones, and perimeter fencing, including locations with little or no hardwired connectivity.

The APS approach

APS deployed self-contained ProVisual™ NEMA Box units configured with solar panels, long-life batteries, AI analytics, and remote monitoring through CHeKT Bridge integration.

A system built for the site

Coverage that can work across connected and off-grid properties.

The value of this approach was not just adding another camera. It was matching the monitoring system to the operating reality of each facility.

Off-grid deployment

Solar panels and long-life batteries enabled the NEMA Box units to operate independently of facility power and hardline internet where needed.

AI-assisted alerts

AI analytics helped distinguish people and vehicles from wildlife and other sources of nuisance activity, improving the quality of alerts.

Remote response

Through CHeKT Bridge integration, alerts could support live video monitoring and response actions such as talk-down, dispatch, or staff notification.

“The goal was to make alerts more actionable—not simply to generate more of them.”

Project questions

Questions storage operators may have.

Can self-storage facilities be monitored without hardwired internet?

In this APS case study, solar-powered ProVisual™ NEMA Box units were deployed for sites with minimal infrastructure. A site evaluation would determine what combination of power, connectivity, placement, and monitoring is appropriate.

What areas can a storage-facility security system cover?

The published project focused on entry gates, loading zones, perimeter fencing, and other areas where after-hours activity created risk. Final coverage depends on the property layout and the desired detection goals.

What happens after an alert is verified?

With live remote monitoring and CHeKT Bridge integration, available response actions can include talk-down, dispatch, or staff notification, depending on the site’s plan and the nature of the event.
