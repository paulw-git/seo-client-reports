Call & Email Notes

Organized campaign observations, decisions, follow-ups, and planning notes from client communications.

November 2026Current priorities

Website transition and holiday planning

  • Holiday gift-card specials were planned for launch on November 1.
  • The Axel widget and related booking/CRM implementation were nearing completion, with timing and coordination still being monitored.
  • Pre-opening studio pages needed content updates and the same changes needed to be carried over to the WordPress site.
  • Location-page and blog-post work was nearing review on the WordPress development site.
October 2026Migration planning

WordPress, Axel, and location-page improvements

  • The website migration from Squarespace to WordPress was planned to improve performance, consistency, templates, and long-term site management.
  • Axel was being integrated to improve booking, customer relationship management, text/email communication, and the client experience.
  • The location-page redesign emphasized a more prominent “New Client Offer” for open studios and “Founding Membership Specials” for pre-opening locations.
  • Mindbody was expected to remain the point-of-sale and reporting platform while the subscription was reviewed for a lower tier.
  • The launch plan called for building and reviewing a staging site before switching the live website during an off-hours window.
  • Three new studio locations were discussed as being in development or opening before year-end.
September 2026Growth and reporting

Franchise-owner support and campaign reporting

  • Franchise-owner reporting and an educational walkthrough of report metrics were planned.
  • Discussion focused on connecting organic traffic, paid traffic, search, social, email, and location-level marketing into one broader growth picture.
  • There was interest in introducing the SEO/webmaster function to the franchise-owner network through an educational call.
  • September was identified as an important seasonal planning point for getting booking, marketing, and location-page improvements in place.
August 2026Content planning

SEO topics, wellness content, and franchise visibility

  • “Sober October” content was approved as a timely campaign topic, with draft completion targeted for late August.
  • Potential content topics included pain relief, infrared sauna, float therapy, contrast therapy, cold plunging, hydration, supplements, product quality, and wellness education.
  • Content should provide useful general information without relying on specific retail brands that may differ by studio.
  • The franchise page was identified as an opportunity for stronger structure, clearer questions and answers, improved headers, and better on-page SEO.
  • A consistent cadence of approximately two blog posts per month was considered appropriate.
June 2026Brand direction

Storytelling, video, and franchise-owner participation

  • Brand messaging centered on health, healing, connection, and the idea of Pure Sweat as a welcoming community.
  • There was interest in helping Candice spend more time communicating, educating, and appearing in video content.
  • Franchise owners could contribute simple client-story and educational videos using repeatable scripts and practical production guidance.
  • A reusable social-media template for franchise owners to share client journeys was discussed.
Earlier campaign notesTechnical SEO

Site quality, redirects, and content improvements

  • A full URL review identified broken links, 404 errors, old blog URLs, inconsistent pages, and no-index tag pages for follow-up.
  • Future URL changes should include 301 redirects, with links checked before scheduled email campaigns are sent.
  • Page-load performance and mobile formatting were recurring technical concerns.
  • Location pages were to be made more consistent, especially pages for studios that were still coming soon.
  • Top-performing pages and blogs were identified as strong candidates for expanded educational content and video support.

Report Definitions

Plain-language definitions for the metrics, concepts, labels, and controls used throughout this SEO campaign report.

Report Controls and General Terms

Month filter

A control used to display data for one month or for all available months.

All time

The combined reporting period shown in the report rather than a single month.

Metric

A measurable value, such as users, sessions, clicks, impressions, or calls.

Trend

The direction or pattern a metric follows across multiple reporting periods.

Organic

Traffic or visibility earned through unpaid search results rather than paid advertising.

URL

The complete web address of a page, resource, or deliverable.

Google Analytics Traffic Data

Users

The number of distinct users who visited the website during the selected period.

Sessions

Visits to the website. One user can generate multiple sessions.

Organic users

Users whose visit was attributed to unpaid search-engine traffic.

Organic sessions

Sessions attributed to unpaid search-engine traffic.

Organic share

The percentage of total users or sessions that came from organic search.

Traffic source

The channel or origin that brought a visitor to the website, such as organic search, direct, referral, or social.

Google AI Search Impressions

AI search feature

A Google Search experience that uses generative AI to provide an answer, overview, or other AI-assisted result.

AI impression

A counted appearance of a site’s result or link within an eligible Google AI search feature.

AI search appearance

The search-result feature or AI surface in which a site was shown.

AI visibility

The extent to which a brand or website is represented in AI-assisted search experiences.

Google Search Console Data

Clicks

The number of times a user clicked a website result from Google Search.

Impressions

The number of times a website result was shown in Google Search.

Click-through rate (CTR)

The percentage of impressions that resulted in a click. It is calculated as clicks divided by impressions.

Average position

The average position of the site’s highest-ranking result when it appeared in Google Search. Lower numbers represent stronger positions.

Search query

The words or phrase a user entered into Google before seeing a website result.

Search page

A website page that appeared in Google Search for one or more queries.

Device

The device category used for a search, commonly desktop, mobile, or tablet.

Country

The country associated with the user’s Google Search activity.

Google Keyword Rankings

Keyword

A word or phrase for which the website is being monitored in search results.

Ranking position

The website’s recorded position in the search results for a tracked keyword.

Average rank

The average recorded position across the selected keywords or reporting period.

Ranking movement

The change in a keyword’s position compared with an earlier reporting period.

Ranking distribution

The number of tracked keywords appearing within position groups such as the top 3, top 10, top 20, or top 100.

Tracked keyword

A keyword selected for ongoing monitoring in the campaign’s ranking data.

Google Business Profile Metrics

Google Business Profile (GBP)

The business listing that can appear in Google Search and Google Maps.

Profile views

The number of times the Business Profile was viewed in Google Search or Maps.

Searches

The number of times users encountered the Business Profile through Google Search discovery.

Website actions

Clicks from the Business Profile to the website.

Calls

Calls initiated through the phone action on the Business Profile.

Directions

Requests for driving directions to the business from the Business Profile.

Website and SEO Campaign Pages

Top Performing Website Pages

Website pages receiving the strongest measured traffic, search visibility, or other selected performance signals.

Page views

The number of times a page was loaded or viewed.

Pages Made for the SEO Campaign

Landing pages, service pages, location pages, or other pages created or tracked as part of the campaign.

Landing page

A page designed to serve a specific search intent, audience, location, service, or campaign goal.

Internal link

A link from one page on the same website to another page on that website.

Search intent

The underlying goal or question behind a user’s search.

Campaign Timeline and Deliverables

Item

A month-by-month campaign activity recorded on the project timeline.

Complete

The listed campaign item has been finished.

In Process

The listed campaign item is currently underway or has not yet been marked complete.

Campaign deliverable

A report, audit, content map, research document, or other campaign resource linked from the timeline.

Campaign month

A calendar month represented in the project history.

Source project board

The shared project-management board used as the source for the timeline’s monthly activities.
